# Personnel Welfare Intelligence System

An AI-driven **Personnel Stress and Welfare Monitoring System** designed for CAPFs, Armed Forces, police organizations, disaster-response forces, and other high-stress uniformed services.

> **Core Principle:** The system identifies emerging welfare risks primarily from existing organizational data. Personnel are **not required to provide personal wellness information** for the core system to function. Voluntary wellness information is an optional enrichment layer.

---

## 1. Problem

Personnel serving in uniformed services operate under physically demanding, psychologically stressful, and potentially hazardous conditions.

Potential welfare pressures can arise from:

- Prolonged deployments
- Irregular and extended duty hours
- Repeated night duties
- Insufficient recovery periods
- Frequent transfers and postings
- Heavy training commitments
- Leave/recovery disruptions
- Manpower shortages
- Sustained operational workload
- Separation from normal routines and support systems

Current identification of stress and welfare concerns can depend heavily on manual observation and self-reporting, which may delay intervention.

The objective is therefore to move from a **reactive welfare process to a proactive and preventive welfare-support framework**, while preserving personnel dignity, confidentiality, privacy, and organizational trust.

---

# 2. Our Core Idea

We do **not** build another simple stress questionnaire, generic AI chatbot, or dashboard that merely displays a risk score.

Instead, we build a **privacy-preserving welfare intelligence layer over existing organizational systems**.

The system first asks:

> **"What does the organization's existing operational data tell us about the individual's current workload, exposure, recovery opportunity, and welfare pressure?"**

Only when appropriate, the system can optionally incorporate information voluntarily provided by the individual.
